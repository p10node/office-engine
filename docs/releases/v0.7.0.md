## Apple Pages, Keynote and Numbers

- `.pages`, `.key` and `.numbers` files (Pages 5, Keynote 6 and Numbers 3 from 2013, and later) open like Office files: `src` (File, Blob, URL), File → Open and drag & drop. They are shown read-only: the worker converts them in memory to DOCX, PPTX or XLSX and the matching view shows the result. Find, print, word count, the slideshow, sheet tabs and *Download as* PDF / HTML / text / CSV all work. *Download as* Word / PowerPoint / Excel saves the converted file. The bar shows a `PAGES`, `KEYNOTE` or `NUMBERS` badge and no Edit button, even with `editable`.
- Pages: paragraph and character styles (named styles become Word styles), fonts and text formatting, alignment, indents and spacing, page breaks, bullet and numbered lists, links, inline images, text boxes and filled shapes, tables, the header and footer (left, center and right parts, page number), footnotes (listed after the text), page size and margins.
- Keynote: slide size and order, skipped slides (hidden), master slide objects drawn under each slide, backgrounds (color, gradient, image), title / body / slide number placeholders, text boxes with lists and links, shapes (preset and custom paths, fill and line), images cropped by their mask, movie poster frames, groups, rotation and flips, tables.
- Numbers: each table becomes a worksheet, named after its sheet (plus the table name when a sheet has several tables). Numbers and dates stay numbers, with an Excel number format matching the Numbers format: currency, percent, scientific, date patterns. Durations, fractions, numbers in other bases, checkboxes, star ratings and pop-up choices show the text Numbers shows. Cells keep their look, plus column widths, row heights (grown to fit wrapped text), merged cells, hidden rows and columns, and the frozen header.
- Tables in all three apps keep merged cells, header rows, cell fills, banded rows and lines (the table style's, plus lines set on the table itself). Cell values use the same number formats.
- Both single files and zipped package folders (`Report.pages/…` inside a ZIP) open. Password-protected files and the iWork '09 XML format give an error saying so.

Known limits: formulas are not converted (cells hold the values Numbers computed). Not converted yet: charts (Keynote shows a placeholder box), equations, comments, tracked changes, Keynote builds, transitions and speaker notes, shadows, text that shrinks to fit, Numbers custom formats (shown as General) and accounting-style currency, the document locale's decimal separators, objects placed on Numbers sheets. Floating objects in Pages are placed in the text flow, not at their exact position.

## Fixes

- xlsx: a number format with a lowercase `am/pm` (or `a/p`) shows `pm`, as Excel does; it showed `PM`.

For integrators: `OpenResult.from` (`client.info.from`) is `'pages'`, `'keynote'` or `'numbers'` for a converted file, and `kind` is the Office format it is shown as. The File → Open picker also offers `.pages`, `.key` and `.numbers`.
