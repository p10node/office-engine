## Fixes

- Editing: the caret is followed again after the editor was detached and attached again (leaving edit mode and coming back, moving to another text box on a slide) while a selection change was still waiting for its frame. Detaching cancelled that frame but kept its handle, so every later selection change was ignored and the editor stopped knowing where the caret was: the page holding the caret was no longer kept mounted while scrolling, and text typed after scrolling away landed at the top of the document instead of at the caret. Found and fixed in #2. Thank you.
